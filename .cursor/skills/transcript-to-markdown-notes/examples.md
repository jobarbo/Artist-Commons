## Good vs avoid

### Good

- Title + Overview only (no `**Speaker:**` / `**Host:**`), then themed `##` sections separated by `---`
- Neutral class notes: ideas stated directly, not "the speaker argues…"
- Paraphrased claims: "Warm connections beat cold outreach almost every time"
- Bold for **curator** vs **gallerist** when contrasting roles
- Blockquote for a principle or example email — not attributed to a named lecturer
- Ends with **Takeaways** or numbered practical summary

### Avoid

- **Speaker attribution:** `**Speaker:** Haiver`, `**Jenn** emphasizes`, "Haiver notes that…"
- **Transcript dump:** leaving `Haiver | 00:00` lines or long unedited monologues
- **Emoji headers:** [quarter_1/lectures/103_lec_visual_analysis/summaries/lecture-wrap-up.md](../../../quarter_1/lectures/103_lec_visual_analysis/summaries/lecture-wrap-up.md) (`🎯 Key Takeaway`) — legacy style
- **Plain text only:** [quarter_1/lectures/105_Art_history_machines/summaries/summary.txt](../../../quarter_1/lectures/105_Art_history_machines/summaries/summary.txt) unless user asks for `.txt`
- **Generic praise sections** with no actionable detail from the session
- **Over-quoting:** more than a few blockquotes per summary

## Naming

- Lectures: `summary.md` in the lecture folder (canonical)
- Reading: `*-summary.md` or `book-summary.md` in the reading folder
- Alternate `lecture-wrap-up.md` is acceptable for presentations but prefer `summary.md` for new work
