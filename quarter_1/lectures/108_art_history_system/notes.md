### key takeaways

- Uncertainty is a constant in human history but our ways of relating to it change -- from ritual and faith , to calculation, to systems and algorithms
- chance is not always the opposite of order.Across history, it shifts from being a creative force, to something suppressed , to something designed into systems
- Authorship emerges together with risk. When divine and traditional guarantees collapse, the artist becomes responsible for meaning -- and for being wrong
- Modernity turns uncertainty into a calculation, princing risk through probablity, insurance and speculation, without eliminating existential doubt
- Scientific revolutions dismantel determinism. QUantum physics shows uncertainty as structural and not a flaw of knowledge. The observer is part of the system.

**Reality is not obliged to be meaningful**
