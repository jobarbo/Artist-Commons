# WE/SYSTEMS LEARN UNCERTAINTY

**generative art is about the boundary between control and unpredictability**

Historical experiment that are akin to generative art

1. **The loss of guarantees**
   - Why the loss of guarantees becomes one of the deepest existential shocks in history.
   - How cultures invent tootls -- ritual, faith, chance, calculation -- to negotiate the unknown.
2. **From Absurdity to Instruction**
   - When randomness stops being chaos and becomes a method.
   - How art begins to design conditions for emergence instead of fixed meanings
3. **When Error Becomes Information**
   - When controls shifts from the artist's hand to rules, instructions, and systems. How authorship dissolves into execution, delegation and feedback
4. **Designing Systems**
   - When form is no longer composed but generated
   - How algorithms, emergence, and on-chain systems redefine what an artwork is...

In these ancient cultures Chance and Chaos were understood as creative tools, as natural or spiritual forces

In cave paintings, they used the caves irregular surfaces to paint the animals, they did not try to force control upon the rock.

But, In the medieval world, Innovations was **Heresy**
**Uncertainty** is resolved in advance through faith

Question: What happens with ego if we work only with pattern or pre-design rules.
