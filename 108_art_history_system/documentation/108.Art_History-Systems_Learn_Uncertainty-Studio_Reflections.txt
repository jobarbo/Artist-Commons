What part of my work am I still trying to fully control — and why do I
need that guarantee? What fear would appear if I allowed that part to
remain undecided?

If my artwork were a system rather than an object, what rules would
actually govern it — and which ones am I pretending are neutral? What
happens if I make those rules visible instead of hiding them inside
form?
Where in my process could an error stop being a failure and start
becoming information?
What do I want to be the author of: the image, or the conditions
under which the image emerges? If I step back, what would continue
acting in my place?
How much uncertainty am I willing to allow — not aesthetically, but
existentially — in my practice right now?
